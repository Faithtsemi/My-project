What we do

Every visit starts with listening — how you use your eyes day to day shapes what we test and what we recommend.

Comprehensive eye examination

Full vision testing and eye health screening, with a plain-language explanation of what we find.

Spectacles and contact lenses

A fitted range of frames and lenses, chosen and checked so they suit your prescription and your face.

Diagnostics

In-depth testing to identify the cause behind vision changes or discomfort, not just a prescription update.

Ocular pathology and disease

Detection and management of eye disease and conditions, with referral to a specialist where needed.

Binocular vision

Testing how well your eyes work together, useful for headaches, double vision or eye strain.

Paediatric optometry

Age-appropriate testing for children, with a calm approach for kids who've never had an exam before.

Low vision and devices

Support and magnification devices for patients whose vision can't be fully corrected with standard lenses.

Driver's licence certificate

Vision testing and certification for your driver's licence application or renewal.

Vision therapy

A structured programme of exercises to improve visual skills like focusing, tracking and coordination.
